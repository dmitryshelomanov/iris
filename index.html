What is Iris?

A pro-minded camera for people who care how a frame feels — not a social feed wrapped around a shutter. Capture photo or video, switch lenses, dial exposure by hand, and bake a look into the file that lands in your library.

Key features

Everything stays on device. No account, no cloud — just the camera you open to shoot.

  • 01

    Multi-lens & zoom

    Switch 0.5× / 1× / tele and glide through zoom on hardware that supports it.

  • 02

    Pro manual controls

    ISO, shutter, white balance, focus, and exposure — when you want full control.

  • 03

    Looks baked in

    Film-inspired presets are applied natively into saved photos and video, not just overlays.

  • 04

    Assist overlays

    Grid, level, histogram, zebra, peaking, and aspect crop while you frame the shot.

  • 05

    Capture presets

    Scene chips, countdown, volume shutter, and defaults that stick across launches.

  • 06

    Iris gallery

    Browse recent shots by type and look, review the last capture, keep an Iris album in Photos.

Capture

Pro controls. Film looks.

Mode, aspect, timer, and look chips sit where your thumbs already are. Presets like Kodak Gold bake into the saved file — not a filter you lose on export.

Library

Browse by look and type.

The Iris gallery keeps recent captures close — filter photos, videos, favorites, and the look you shot with.

Review

Before / after and EXIF.

Open a shot to compare looks, read ISO and shutter, favorite keepers, and share — without leaving Iris.

Defaults

Defaults that stick.

Aspect, quality, HDR, stabilization, look strength — set once, shoot for weeks.

Looks, baked in

Same scene — different looks written into the file.

In the app

Real UI from the camera, gallery, review, and settings.

Open source. On-device.
